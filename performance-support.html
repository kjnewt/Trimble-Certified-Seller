Seller Performance Support

When one of your numbers dips, you get a short, specific plan built around the habits that move it. No blanket course, no guesswork, and your manager is in it with you.

Specific

Support targets the one skill behind the dip, using your own calls and deals.

In your control

Every pack leads with weekly habits you fully control, not a result you can only hope for.

Developmental

An Impact Pack is not a performance warning. It is focused help to get your momentum back.

The three signals

Results tell us something changed. Habits are how you change it.

Performance support reads three kinds of measures together. Each one answers a different question.

Results

Did the number move?

Your commercial outcomes at the end of a period. A dip here is the first signal that support could help.

  • Win rate
  • Booked ACV
  • Lost ACV
  • Quota attainment
In-flight quality

Where is it breaking down?

How well active deals and calls are being run. This is where the specific skill shows up.

  • Gong discovery score
  • MEDDPICC completeness
  • Multi-threading depth
  • Days per stage
Activities

What can I do this week?

Daily and weekly effort that is fully in your control. These become the habit checklist in your pack.

  • Talk-to-listen balance
  • Open questions per call
  • Pre-call plans and Upfront Contracts
  • Opportunity updates within 48 hours
Why all three: one number on its own can mislead. Losing a single large deal can drop a win rate without any skill gap behind it. An Impact Pack is only built when a results dip lines up with a quality gap on active deals and a habit you can see on calls.
How it works

From signal to recovery in seven steps

The same loop runs every time, so you always know what happens next and who does what.

Signal

A weekly Scorecard check compares your results and in-flight quality with the baseline for your role.

Domo and Salesforce

Diagnose

Seller Compass, using Gong and Salesforce

Seller Compass

Two packs

You receive a Seller Impact Pack. Your manager receives a matching Coaching Pack. Both arrive by email.

You and your manager

Align

You and your manager meet for 30 minutes, within 48 hours, and agree on the top two habits to start with.

You and your manager

Weekly habits

You work the habits on live calls and deals, with a short weekly call review together.

You, with coaching

Three check-ins confirm the habits, then the quality, then the result. When the result recovers, the pack closes.

Feedback

30, 60, 90

Effort first, then quality, then results

Results take a quarter to show. The check-ins start with what you control, so you can see progress long before the number moves.

30

Habit check

Are the weekly habits happening?

  • Weekly habits completed
  • Talk time at or under 45% on Gong calls
  • Ten or more open questions per discovery call
  • MEDDPICC fields updated within 48 hours
60

Quality check

Are calls and deals being run better?

  • Gong discovery score at 3.8 of 5 or higher
  • MEDDPICC complete on 85% or more of qualified deals
  • Three or more contacts engaged per deal
  • Upfront Contract set on every call
90

Result recovery

Did the number come back?

  • Win rate back toward the role benchmark
  • Stage 2 deals moving again
  • Attainment back on track
  • Impact Pack closed
Learn, Verify, Ready: your manager confirms progress in three stages. Learn: you complete the short learning. Verify: you show the skill in a 15 minute role play. Ready: you show it on three live customer calls in a row.

Guardrails

Built to be fair and safe to use

Performance support only works if sellers trust it. These rules protect that trust.

Confidential

An Impact Pack and its coaching notes stay between the seller, the direct manager and the enablement team supporting them.

No peer rankings

Seller packs compare you with the benchmark for your role, never with named teammates or a cohort leaderboard.

Forward-looking coaching

Manager packs are scoped to their direct report and focus on the skill ahead, not on a history of missed quota.

Evidence, not opinion

A pack starts from data on calls and deals, so the conversation is about observable habits rather than a judgment of the person.
