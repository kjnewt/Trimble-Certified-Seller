Impact Plans

When a KPI dips, support gets specific

Below-target performance leads to competency-based remediation: the exact badges mapped to that KPI, with your manager in the loop.

Signal

A Scorecard KPI stays below target for the agreed period.

Diagnose

The KPI map names the competencies most linked to that result.

Assign

Only those badges are assigned, plus one manager-observed call.

Exit

You exit the plan when the KPI recovers, not when a course is complete.

Built on recognized standards

Fair, defensible, and worth earning

TCS is designed against the same principles used by professional certification bodies, scaled to the stakes of each credential.

ISO/IEC 17024

Job task analysis defines each role; assessors are impartial; every rule is documented.

NCCA principles

Published eligibility, retake and appeals policies; regular item analysis.

Action Mapping

Every badge starts with a business result and an observable action, then realistic practice.

Kirkpatrick + Gagne

Each experience is built on the nine events of instruction and measured through behavior and results.
