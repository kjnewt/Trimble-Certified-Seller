How it works

Your roadmap to Trimble Certified Seller

Three steps take you from your first day to certified.

1. Competency

Four skill areas define how top Trimble sellers work: Product and Industry, Consultative Selling, Tools and Workflows, and Sales Process.

→

2. Trimble Certified Seller Curriculum

Each badge gives you the learning, the practice and the proof for one skill. You do most of it on your own deals, not in a classroom.

→

3. KPI

Your Seller Scorecard shows the payoff: Booked ACV, Attainment, Win Rate, Average Deal Size and more.

The principle: Seller behaviors drive results, and the KPI measures competency success.
